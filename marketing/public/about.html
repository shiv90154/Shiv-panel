About

One panel, run on infrastructure you actually own.

ShivDomains is a hosting control panel — not a reseller account on somebody else's shared server.

Most hosting is a shared platform: your mail, your site and your database all sit on infrastructure you don't control, behind a dashboard someone else designed. ShivDomains takes the opposite approach — it's a full control panel that runs on your own server, so the infrastructure underneath is actually yours.

Under the hood it's built on well-established, standard software: Postfix and Dovecot for mail, Rspamd for spam filtering and DKIM signing, Caddy for automatic HTTPS, PostgreSQL for the panel's own data, and standard runtimes (static, PHP, Node, Python) for websites. Nothing proprietary — if you ever wanted to move off it, your mail and files are in standard formats any other server could read.

It's built and run independently, not by a large hosting company — so when you get in touch, you're talking directly to the person who built it, not a support queue.
